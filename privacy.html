Data used

LT2 Auto Capture may use a Discord username, Roblox username, gameplay clips, report information, YouTube channel information, video titles, and Google OAuth credentials.

Why data is used

The data is used to record clips, create reports, upload approved videos, and find the next report number.

Storage

Files and credentials may be stored on the computer running the program.

Sharing

Approved videos and their information are sent to YouTube. Report information may be sent to the configured report form.

Deletion

Users can delete local files, remove saved credentials, delete uploaded videos, and revoke Google access.
